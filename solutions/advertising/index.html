The advertising problem

The winning creative is hidden inside the variation loop

Advertising teams rarely need one beautiful generation. They need enough controlled variation to discover what works, then the capacity to produce approved adaptations across placements, formats, markets, and deadlines.

Per-request video APIs can become expensive when every approved asset requires many discarded attempts. Rate limits collide with campaign launches, product imagery crosses external systems, and changing model behavior makes brand review harder to reproduce. The useful economic unit is the approved creative, not the generated clip.

API cost alternative

When dedicated capacity is worth evaluating

Open-weight deployment is not automatically cheaper than Seedance, Runway, Kling, MiniMax, or another hosted API. It becomes a serious option when the team has sustained generation volume, repeatable formats, material rejection spend, private asset requirements, or campaign peaks that justify reserved throughput.

Nuva Lab compares the current provider workflow against a private candidate using the same briefs and review standard. Quality comes first. We then measure accepted variants per day, complete-media latency, retry rate, utilization, operator effort, and fully loaded cost per approved asset.

Qualification gateProceed only when the candidate meets the brand quality bar and produces a measurable advantage in approval rate, deadline capacity, privacy, control, or total accepted-output cost.

Deployment and licensing

Open infrastructure without vague rights claims

The system can use FastVideo, an independent Apache-2.0 open-source framework, alongside MiniMax H3, FastH3, or another qualified open-weight video model. The framework license does not replace the license attached to model weights, derivatives, training inputs, product references, music, talent, or final campaign usage.

Before deployment, Nuva Lab documents model authorization, territory, intended commercial use, derivative rights, data handling, and customer asset provenance. Model portability is part of the design so a campaign operation is not forced to treat one checkpoint as permanent infrastructure.
